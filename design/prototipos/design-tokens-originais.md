## Brand & Style

This design system treats shared group finances not as an awkward accounting chore, but as a communal, energetic social ritual. It rejects both clinical, sterile neo-banking templates and overly playful cartoon aesthetics. Instead, it pulls directly from contemporary editorial print design, poster typography, and expressive packaging.

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Unapologetically Direct:** Financial splits must be legible in a glance. Numbers are bold, structured, and presented with confident scale.
- **Social & Conversational:** The tone is energetic, tactile, and warm—removing the friction, social anxiety, and awkwardness of debt settlement among peers.
- **Tactile Editorial Craft:** Surfaces feel like heavy matte stock, layered paper cutouts, and printed matter rather than sterile digital layers.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Editorial Meets Neo-Grotesque:** High-contrast typographic scale paired with razor-sharp structural rules, explicit hairline borders, and graphic blocks of high-energy color.
- **Surface Reality:** Warm cream paper foundations layered with pure white punchy modules, grounded by dense ink-graphite lettering and jolted awake by cobalt, hyper-tangerine, and electric lime accents.
- **Intentional Density:** Clean vertical rhythm with rhythmically tight line heights, expressive tabular numerals, and dynamic block arrangements.

## Colors

The palette relies on a tactile, warm foundation offset by sharp saturated primaries and electric accents. High-contrast ink typography ensures flawless outdoor readability and rapid scanning during fast-paced social outings.

### Primary Palette
- **Primary (Azul-Cobalto — `#2546F0`):** The operational engine. Used for primary calls to action, total split summaries, user share indicators, and active navigational anchors.
- **Secondary / Accent (Laranja Vibrante — `#FF7139`):** The social spark. Used for balance debt alerts, unsettled tabs, action badges, and dynamic group highlights.
- **Tertiary / Micro-Accent (Verde-Limão — `#D9F36A`):** The signal light. Used sparingly for settled balances, success confirmations, "You are owed" indicators, and high-energy sticker tags. Always paired with `#20211E` text to guarantee AA/AAA contrast.
- **Neutral Primary (Grafite — `#20211E`):** Heavy printing ink. Used for headlines, high-priority labels, crisp stroke borders, and critical numerical data. Never washed out.
- **Neutral Muted (Muted Text — `#6B6963`):** Editorial sub-copy, metadata, category labels, and secondary calculation context.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base (Creme — `#F7F3EA`):** The foundational screen surface. Emulates heavy, unbleached tactile cardstock.
- **Surface Alt / Well (Creme Dim — `#EFE9DC`):** Recessed containers, tally inputs, inactive segment track wells, and item breakdown blocks.
- **Surface Pure (White — `#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast highlight cards, dynamic receipts, floating sheets, and focus badges.
- **Dividers & Strokes (`#20211E` at 100% or 12%):** Key containers use distinct `1.5px` or `2px` solid `#20211E` borders, while structured internal lists use hairline dividers (`rgba(32, 33, 30, 0.12)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy delivers punchy editorial pacing:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines, Amounts, Badges, Labels):** Offers geometric quirkiness, tight letterforms, and confident structural stability. Used for all numbers, currency values, navigation bar anchors, and section intros.
- **Manrope (Body, Descriptions, Activity Streams):** A geometric-grotesque hybrid that maintains crisp clarity at small sizes without fighting Space Grotesk. Used across itemized lists, split receipts, participant listings, and conversational form instructions.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tabular Figures:** All financial splits, tab tallies, and member ratios must use proportional or tabular numerical styling to ensure straight column alignment down list views.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** Category tags and calculation labels utilize `label-caps` in uppercase styling with deliberate tracking (`0.08em`), establishing visual anchor points on the warm background.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout system operates on an editorial fluid grid optimized primarily for vertical hand-held mobile interaction, with clean modular adaptations for tablet split views.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Mobile Grid (Base):** 4-column fluid layout with a mandatory `1rem` outer margin (`margin-mobile`) and `0.75rem` gutters (`gutter-sm`). 
- **Tablet / Responsive Expand:** Expands to an 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins (`margin-tablet`) and `1rem` gutters (`gutter`), arranging the summary ledger and item allocation onto adjacent editorial columns.
- **Rhythm & Stacking:** Built strictly on an 8pt spatial cadence with 4pt half-steps for micro-components (chips, badges, avatars). Content blocks are stacked like printed cards with `space-md` (`1rem`) or `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) gaps.
- **Edge Alignment:** Full-bleed color headers and edge-to-edge list separators maintain direct structural anchors to the screen boundaries, avoiding floating island card fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids corporate blurred shadows, tinted dropshadow layers, and frosted glassmorphism. Depth is achieved purely through flat physical layering, structural contrast, and intentional tactile offsets:

### 1. Structural Line Work
Hierarchy is established via sharp `1.5px` solid `#20211E` ink borders. Primary modules rest directly on `#F7F3EA` canvas bounded by clean strokes rather than nebulous blur filters.

### 2. The Hard Editorial Offset ("Poster Drop")
For elevated interactive triggers (such as primary CTA buttons, floating bottom action bars, or active receipt drawer handles), the system employs a zero-blur hard offset shadow:
- **X / Y Offset:** `3px 3px` or `4px 4px`.
- **Blur Radius:** `0px`.
- **Shadow Color:** `#20211E` (100% solid opacity).
- **Press State:** On active tap/touch, the element translates down and right by `2px` or `3px`, collapsing the hard shadow to produce a mechanical, tactile depression.

### 3. Surface Stacking (Paper Layering)
- **Base Canvas:** `#F7F3EA` (Warm Creme).
- **Recessed Surface:** `#EFE9DC` with no stroke or a `1px` recessed hairline border for input wells and grouped item brackets.
- **Raised Module:** `#FFFFFF` with a `1.5px` solid `#20211E` stroke.
- **Accent Overlay:** Solid `#D9F36A` or `#FF7139` chips floating over white cards with crisp `#20211E` outlines.

## Shapes

The design uses tight, deliberate geometric curvature (`roundedness: 1` — Soft). Shapes recall punch-punched cards, ticket stubs, and printed editorial grids rather than bubbly app components.

### Radius Scale
- **Base Components (`rounded` / `4px` - `0.25rem`):** Badges, input checkboxes, split-ratio indicators, and small member avatar frames.
- **Modules & Cards (`rounded-lg` / `8px` - `0.5rem`):** Group bills, receipt item cards, bottom sheets, and modal viewports.
- **Action Triggers (`rounded-xl` / `12px` - `0.75rem`):** Tap targets, primary button modules, and calculation tally bars.
- **Pill Exception (Full Round):** Exclusively reserved for status micro-tags, monetary debt chips ("Pendente", "Pago"), and avatar initials circles to produce dynamic contrast against angular card modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Split Now, Add Expense, Settle):** Background `#2546F0`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Space Grotesk` (`label-md`), `1.5px` solid `#20211E` border, `rounded-xl` (`12px`), with a `3px 3px 0px #20211E` hard shadow. Active press state removes the hard shadow via `transform: translate(2px, 2px)`.
- **Secondary Action (Even Split, Assign Specific Item):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#20211E`, `1.5px` solid `#20211E` border, no shadow or subtle `2px 2px 0px #20211E`.
- **Destructive / Urgent (Clear Tab, Delete):** Background `#FF7139`, text `#20211E` or `#FFFFFF`, `1.5px` border `#20211E`.
- **Micro / Utility (Add Person, Quick +1):** Background `#EFE9DC`, text `#20211E`, border `1px` solid `rgba(32, 33, 30, 0.2)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Solid `#D9F36A` background with `#20211E` text for "Tudo Pago / Quitou"; solid `#FF7139` with `#FFFFFF` text for "Você Deve / Pendente". High-impact, all-caps `11px` typography with `0.08em` tracking.
- **Category Filter Chips (Bar, Restaurante, Mercado, Viagem):** Background `#FFFFFF` when unselected with `1px` `#20211E` border; when active, fills with `#2546F0` (text `#FFFFFF`) or `#20211E` (text `#FFFFFF`). Accompanied by a colored geometric dot indicator (`#FF7139`, `#D9F36A`, `#2546F0`).

### Lists & Split Breakdown
- **Itemized Expense Item:** Horizontal layout on `#FFFFFF` card or bare `#F7F3EA` ground. Hairline bottom border (`rgba(32, 33, 30, 0.12)`). Left side displays participant avatar stack and item name in `Manrope` (`body-lg`); right side features bold currency values in `Space Grotesk` (`currency-md`).
- **Payer & Owed Breakdown:** Alternating background rows using subtle `#EFE9DC` blocks to distinguish who covered the upfront payment versus the remaining group shares.

### Inputs & Financial Entry
- **Large Value Inputs (Total Bill Amount):** Borderless or bottom-stroke-only field with large `Space Grotesk` tabular numerals (`currency-display`). Currency symbol "R$" fixed in muted `#6B6963`.
- **Form Controls:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#EFE9DC`, `1.5px` solid `#20211E` frame, `rounded` (`4px`). Focus state highlights with an electric `#2546F0` border and `2px 2px 0px #20211E` shadow.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Toggles
- **Custom Split Selector Checkbox:** Square `20px` container with `rounded` (`4px`), `1.5px` solid `#20211E`. Checked state fills `#D9F36A` with a heavy black graphic checkmark (`#20211E`).
- **Percentage/Fraction Pill Radios:** Segmented bar contained in `#EFE9DC` track. Active pill clicks into place with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#20211E`, and sharp black text.

### Cards & Container Modules
- **Summary "The Tab" Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, bounded by a `2px` solid `#20211E` perimeter, nested `rounded-lg` (`8px`), featuring a hard `4px 4px 0px #20211E` offset shadow. Card headers use full-width color-blocked strips (e.g., solid `#2546F0` band with `#FFFFFF` title).
- **Settlement Receipt Module:** Monospaced-inspired tabular structure with serrated or notched ticket dividers separating the running subtotal, tip/service percentage, and calculated per-person balance.
